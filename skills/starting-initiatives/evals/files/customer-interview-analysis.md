# Customer Interview Analysis

## Provisional intent

### Outcome

Help a researcher turn multiple customer interviews into traceable findings without losing disagreement or uncertainty.

### Subject

Researchers and product teams analyzing customer interviews.

### Constraints and anti-goals

D7Y should support discovery rather than generate polished research claims without provenance.

## Primary uncertainty

Whether structured agent synthesis can save meaningful time without flattening contradictory evidence.

## Current understanding

### Evidence

User-provided observation: manual synthesis across several interviews is slow.

### Assumptions

Contradictions and provenance are important enough that existing summarization workflows are inadequate.

## Current state

The opportunity is active. The next move is to compare current interview-analysis workflows and define evidence-preservation requirements.
